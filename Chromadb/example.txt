The Clockmaker's Secret

In the quiet village of Oakhaven, there lived an old man named Elias. He was known as the finest clockmaker in the land, but his most famous creation was not a clock at all. It was a small, silver bird that sat atop his workbench.

People traveled from far and wide to see the bird. It didn't just chirp; it sang melodies that seemed to change based on the listener's mood. If you were sad, it sang a comforting lullaby. If you were joyful, it performed a lively jig.

One day, a greedy merchant offered Elias a chest of gold for the bird. "What is the secret?" the merchant demanded. "Is it powered by magic or rare jewels?"

Elias simply smiled. "It is powered by attention," he replied. "I built it to listen. The more carefully you listen to the world around you, the more music you will find in it."

The merchant left, frustrated and empty-handed. But Elias and his silver bird remained, reminding everyone that sometimes the most valuable things are not made of gold, but of the moments we take to truly listen.
